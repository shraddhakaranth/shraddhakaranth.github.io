Teaching

I aspire to be a teacher-scholar. I love mentoring and helping young scientists grow, and I started teaching at the University of Maryland in 2016. I was a teaching assistant for four courses, with class sizes from about 20 to about 200 students, and as a post-doctoral researcher I mentored undergraduate and graduate students. I want to keep research and teaching together in my career.
