About Me

I am a food scientist. My expertise is in food microbiology, microbial risk assessment, food quality and safety assessment, immunobiology, and machine learning and statistical analysis. I received my Ph.D. in Food Science from the University of Maryland, College Park, and then did 2.5 years of post-doctoral research in the same field. I have used statistical methods and machine learning to analyze microbial whole genome sequencing data for risk assessment. That work earned me multiple academic awards and brought about $700K in research funding to my lab. I have published 13 journal articles and 2 book chapters with 983 total citations. I have also presented at international conferences and mentored students.


P.S: I am currently on a sabbatical raising two tiny humans (3 and 5) in NYC. It's been more intense compared to academia, but with fewer footnotes. Now I'm back, recharged and buzzing with fresh ideas (and maybe some Kpop Demon Hunters music 😊).
